The dough

Three days for one pizza

We use four ingredients in the dough: flour, water, salt and a pinch of yeast. The time does the rest.

  1. Mix and rest

    Flour, water, salt and yeast are mixed by hand and rested until smooth and slack.

    Day 1 · 20 min

  2. Cold ferment

    Balls go into the cooler for 72 hours. The slow rise builds flavour and a light, blistered crust.

    Day 1 to 4 · 72 hr

  3. Stretch by hand

    No rolling pin. Each ball is opened by hand so the edge keeps its air.

    To order · 1 min

  4. Fire

    Into the wood-fired oven at 485°C, turned once, out when the crust leopard-spots.

    To order · 90 sec
